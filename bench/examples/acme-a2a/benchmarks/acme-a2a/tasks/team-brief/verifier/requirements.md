# team-brief: what the expected answer holds, and why

`docs/handbook.md` section 3 says orders are packed in Leeds and carried by
Parcelwing, so `answer.yaml` expects "Leeds; Parcelwing" (case, punctuation
and spacing ignored). The plausible wrong answers are the York display shop,
the returns courier Swiftbox, and the city alone. In the team experiment
the entry agent delegates the two halves to two other agents through the
agent gateway; the answer is graded the same way.
