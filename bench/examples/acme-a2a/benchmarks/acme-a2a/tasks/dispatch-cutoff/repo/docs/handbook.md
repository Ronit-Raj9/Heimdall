# Acme Tea customer support handbook (fictional)

Acme Tea sells loose-leaf tea in tins by post. Everything in this handbook is
invented for the Heimdall acme-a2a example.

## 1. Refunds

- Unopened tins can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery.
- Opened tins can be returned within 14 days, for store credit only.
- Gift sets bought in the winter sale can be returned within 60 days.

## 2. Dispatch

- Orders placed before 14:00 (UK time) on a working day leave the same day.
- Orders placed after the cut-off leave the next working day.
- Saturday orders placed before 12:00 leave on Monday morning.

## 3. Warehouse and couriers

- Every order is packed at the warehouse in Leeds. The York shop keeps
  display stock only.
- Parcels are carried by Parcelwing. Swiftbox carries only returns.
