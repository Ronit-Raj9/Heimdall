# Contract summary: Northwind Archive (cloud backup)

Prepared by Acme procurement, 12 May 2026.

- Term: 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2028, renews yearly unless cancelled with 60
  days' notice.
- Scope: nightly backups of the finance and HR file shares, 30-day
  retention, quarterly restore test.
- Price: EUR 1,840 per month, fixed for the first year, then indexed.
- Account manager: Elif Demir, elif.demir@northwind-archive.example.
- Support: tickets by email to support@northwind-archive.example or through
  the customer portal. Priority 1 tickets get a first response within 1
  hour, around the clock; priority 2 within 4 business hours; priority 3
  within 2 business days.
- Service credit: 5% of the monthly fee for each full hour of a missed
  priority 1 response, capped at 30%.
- Data stays in the EU (Frankfurt and Amsterdam data centres).
- Our contact for the contract: Marco Ferri, IT operations.
