# Make the primary button blue and centred

The account settings page is in `site/` (`index.html` and `style.css`).
Design review asked for one change to its primary button, the "Save
changes" button (class `primary`):

- background colour `#1d4ed8`, text colour white (`#ffffff`);
- centred horizontally in its row at the bottom of the card, instead of
  sitting at the left.

Change only `site/style.css`. Do not change `index.html`, the button's size,
font or padding, or anything else on the page.
