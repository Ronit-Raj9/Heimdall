# Brand brief: Larkspur Ferries (fictional client)

Prepared by Acme Design for the spring identity refresh.

## Palette

Use these five colours, in this order, everywhere the palette is shown.

| # | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Harbor Navy | #1F3A5F |
| 2 | Sandstone | #D8C3A5 |
| 3 | Signal Coral | #E85D4A |
| 4 | Moss | #5B7F4A |
| 5 | Paper White | #F7F4EE |

Paper White is the page colour; give its swatch a thin outline so it shows
on white paper.

## Type

Headlines in a geometric sans, body text in a humanist sans. Not part of
the swatch sheet.
