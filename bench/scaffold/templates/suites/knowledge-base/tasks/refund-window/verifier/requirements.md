# refund-window: what the expected answer holds, and why

The customer opened the item, so the opened-item rule applies.
`refund-policy.md` in the kb documents gives 14 days for opened items and
30 days for unopened ones, so `answer.yaml` expects 14. The plausible wrong
answers are the unopened window (30) and the warranty period from
`warranty.md` (90).
