# Travel policy (excerpt)

Version 7, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to every employee travelling on
company business.

## 1. Booking

Book travel through the travel desk at least 14 days ahead where possible.
Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy above that.

## 2. Hotels

The nightly room rate, before taxes and breakfast, may not exceed the cap
for the city. Cities not listed use the country cap.

| City | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| Berlin | EUR 160 |
| Munich | EUR 175 |
| Geneva | CHF 180 |
| Zurich | CHF 240 |
| London | GBP 210 |

Country caps: Germany EUR 140, Switzerland CHF 170, United Kingdom GBP 150.

Stays of seven nights or more must be booked as serviced apartments where
available.

## 3. Meals

Meals are reimbursed at cost up to EUR 60 (CHF 70, GBP 55) per day.
