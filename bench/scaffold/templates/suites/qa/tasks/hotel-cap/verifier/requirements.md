# hotel-cap: what the expected answer holds, and why

The question asks for the most a traveller may spend per night on a hotel
room in Zurich, in Swiss francs. `docs/travel-policy.md` section 2 lists
Zurich at CHF 240, so `answer.yaml` expects the number 240. The plausible
wrong answers are Geneva's cap (180) and the Swiss country cap (170).
